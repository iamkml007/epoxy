FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your epoxy project.

How long does epoxy take to cure?
+

Most projects are walkable within 24–48 hours and fully cured in 5–7 days. Full chemical resistance develops over 7–14 days. We'll provide a detailed timeline for your specific project.

Is epoxy safe for kitchens and food areas?
+

Yes! Once fully cured, our food-safe epoxy formulations are FDA-compliant and ideal for countertops, bar tops, and food preparation surfaces. We use only premium, non-toxic resins for these applications.

How long does an epoxy floor last?
+

A professionally installed epoxy floor typically lasts 10–20 years with proper maintenance. Our Signature and Luxury packages come with lifetime warranties. Regular cleaning and occasional recoating extend lifespan significantly.

Can you match my existing color scheme?
+

Absolutely. We offer custom color matching using RAL, Pantone, and physical samples. Our team can also create metallic, pearlescent, and 3D effects for truly unique finishes.

Do you offer free estimates?
+

Yes — all estimates are completely free with no obligation. We'll visit your site, discuss your vision, and provide a transparent itemized quote within 48 hours.

What areas do you serve?
+

We serve the entire metropolitan region and surrounding areas within a 150-mile radius. For larger commercial projects, we travel nationwide. Contact us with your location for confirmation.
